Privacy Policy

Your fasts and prayers stay yours.

This policy explains what the Islamic Fasting Calendar app for iPhone (“the app”, “we”) collects, why, and who it is shared with.

Last updated: October 13, 2026 · applies to version 1.0.9 and later

In short

  • No account and no sign-up. We don't know your name or email unless you write to us.
  • Your fasting records, journal, bookmarks, tasbih counts and settings are stored only on your iPhone.
  • Your location is used to work out prayer times. We don't keep it on any server of ours.
  • Free users see non-personalized ads from Google AdMob. Premium users see no ads.
  • We don't sell your data and we don't track you across other apps.

What the app uses

Location

What
Your device's coordinates, only if you allow location access.
Why
To calculate prayer, suhoor and iftar times for where you are, and to show your city's name.
Shared with
The AlAdhan prayer-times service (coordinates, to return the day's times), Apple (to turn coordinates into a city name) and Google AdMob (approximate region, for ads).
Your choice
You can decline location access and pick your city by hand instead.

Device identifier

What
An anonymous identifier for your device or app install.
Why
To show ads (Google AdMob) and to keep track of a Premium purchase (RevenueCat).
Not used for
Identifying you, or tracking you across other companies' apps and websites.

Purchases

What
Whether you have bought Premium, and the purchase record from Apple.
Why
To unlock Premium, remove ads, and restore your purchase on a new device.
Shared with
RevenueCat, which manages purchases for us. Payment itself is handled by Apple; we never see your card details.

Ads

What
Ad requests and whether an ad was shown or tapped.
Why
Ads keep the app free.
How
The app only requests non-personalized ads from Google AdMob, so ads are not based on your interests or history.

Stored only on your iPhone

The following never leave your device. We have no copy of them and cannot see them.

  • Fasts you planned, kept or need to make up
  • Journal entries and daily reflections
  • Quran bookmarks and reading progress
  • Favorite duas and tasbih counts
  • Zakat and charity records
  • Your language, calculation method, notification and Adhan settings
  • The prayer times and daily duas shown in your widgets, which are shared only between the app and its widgets on the same phone

Prayer and fasting reminders, including the optional Adhan sound, are scheduled on your iPhone. No server sends them.

Your choices

  • Location: turn it off in iPhone Settings and choose your city in the app.
  • Notifications: turn prayer, fasting and Adhan alerts on or off in the app's Settings.
  • Ads: Premium removes all ads.
  • Deleting your data: deleting the app removes everything it stored. We have no server-side copy to delete.

Children

The app can be used at any age without giving personal information. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the date at the top.
